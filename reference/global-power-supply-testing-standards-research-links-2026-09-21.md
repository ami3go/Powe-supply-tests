## Scope and provenance

This appendix records the **preserved source URLs that were reviewed or retained in the research output** for the global power-supply-testing standards study.

It is intentionally more precise than claiming to be a complete browser-history export. The research workflow does not retain every transient search-results page, redirect, preview or intermediate discovery page. Therefore:

- URLs below are links that can be traced to the research output or its authoritative-source set.
- Duplicates have been removed.
- Links are grouped by standards body / industry where practical.
- A link being listed here does **not** mean the full copyrighted normative standard was freely accessible.
- Commercial/paywalled pages were used only for public metadata/scope unless lawful access to more material was available.
- No classified, secret, export-controlled, NDA-protected, leaked or unlawfully obtained material is included.

## Traceability rule for future research

For subsequent standards research, every reviewed source should be logged at discovery time with at least:

```text
URL
page/document title
issuing body / publisher
language
access date
standard(s) referenced
edition/year claimed
source type: primary / national adoption / secondary
access: public / commercial / restricted
used in final matrix: yes/no
reason for inclusion or rejection
confidence
```

Recommended machine-readable companion fields:

```yaml
- url: https://example.org/standard-record
  title: Example standard record
  organization: Example body
  language: en
  accessed: 2026-09-21
  standard_id: EXAMPLE-123
  edition: 2026
  source_type: primary
  access: public-metadata
  used_in_matrix: true
  disposition: accepted
  confidence: high
```

This will allow the next deep-search pass to maintain a genuine **source ledger**, including useful rejected sources, rather than reconstructing provenance after the research is complete.
