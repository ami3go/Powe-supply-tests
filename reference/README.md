## Provenance note

The source ledgers record URLs preserved from the research work. They should not be interpreted as a perfect browser-history reconstruction of transient search-result, redirect or session pages that were not retained. The multilingual article index is a broad curated survey rather than a claim that every relevant page on the public Internet has been enumerated. Future research should log each reviewed URL as it is evaluated, including rejected or secondary sources, when full auditability is required.
