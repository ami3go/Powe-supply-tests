## Provenance note

The source ledgers record URLs preserved from the research work. They should not be interpreted as a perfect browser-history reconstruction of transient search-result, redirect or session pages that were not retained. The multilingual article index is a broad curated survey rather than a claim that every relevant page on the public Internet has been enumerated. The function-oriented synthesis records that all 131 indexed URLs were individually attempted during review; where a public page was not retrievable, classification relies on retained title/topic metadata and, for localized duplicate content, an accessible equivalent source. Future research should log each reviewed URL as it is evaluated, including rejected or secondary sources, when full auditability is required.
