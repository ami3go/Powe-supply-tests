# Electronic Power — Industry Coverage Map

**Date:** 2026-09-21  
**Purpose:** Provide a broad industry taxonomy showing where electronic power supplies, converters, chargers, inverters, UPS systems, DC/DC converters, AC/DC supplies, battery power systems and power-conditioning electronics are used. This document is intended to guide future standards-search coverage for the Power Supply Tests project.

- data-centre and rack-level power systems;
- mining and underground equipment;
- hazardous-area / explosion-protected equipment;
- utility and substation DC power;
- renewable-energy power conversion;

- industrial process and petrochemical power electronics;
- charging infrastructure and high-power DC conversion.

## Scope note

This is an industry-coverage map, not a standards-compliance list. Individual sectors can require multiple overlapping standard families covering electrical safety, EMC, input-power quality, transients, environmental qualification, reliability, battery safety, functional safety, fault tolerance and regulatory certification.
