# Automotive Power Supply Validation Test Matrix

_Last updated: 2026-09-20_

## Scope

This matrix is intended for validation of an automotive direct-current power supply, direct-current converter, or electronic control unit power stage. The available bench includes a Keysight N6700 modular power system fitted with an N6775A power module and an N6791A electronic-load module, plus a digital oscilloscope and thermal chamber.

> **Important:** this is a planning matrix, not a substitute for the licensed standard or customer or vehicle-manufacturer component requirement specification. Exact severity, waveform, source impedance, repetitions, functional-status requirement, sample count and acceptance limits shall be taken from the applicable contract specification and standard edition.

## Installed N6700 hardware

- **Keysight N6775A:** programmable direct-current source, 0 to 60 volts, up to 5 amperes and 300 watts. It supports remote sensing, programmable voltage/current, list/arbitrary-waveform operation and integrated voltage/current measurement. It is not regenerative and cannot generate negative input voltage. In a low-profile N6700 mainframe it should be treated primarily as a programmable source with direct-current measurement, not as a replacement for a high-bandwidth oscilloscope.
- **Keysight N6791A:** programmable direct-current electronic-load module, up to 60 volts, 20 amperes and 100 watts. It supports constant-current, constant-voltage, constant-resistance and constant-power modes, adjustable slew rate, list/arbitrary-waveform operation and synchronized voltage/current digitization.
- **N6700 external logging:** voltage/current data can be collected over Standard Commands for Programmable Instruments and stored by the control computer. A separate general-purpose electrical data logger is therefore normally unnecessary. The internal external-log buffer is only about 20 seconds, so the control computer must retrieve data continuously for long tests.
- **Precision caveat:** the N6775A is a general-purpose source, not a precision source-measure unit. Keep an external precision digital multimeter when the required uncertainty is tighter than the N6700 module specifications, for independent verification, or for circuit nodes that the N6700 cannot measure.
- **Sleep-current caveat:** the N6775A current-measurement resolution is in the milliampere range, so microampere-level closed-circuit or sleep-current measurements still require a high-dynamic-range ammeter or source-measure unit.

### Present setup status

- **Available** — the installed N6700 modules and existing oscilloscope/thermal chamber can normally execute the test.
- **Partial** — the N6700 is useful for stimulus, load or measurement, but another fixture or instrument is required for the complete test.
- **Additional equipment required** — dedicated transient, electromagnetic-compatibility, environmental, high-voltage or other specialized equipment is required.

## What the N6700 replaces in this bench

With the N6775A and N6791A installed, a separate general-purpose programmable input supply, separate electronic load and separate electrical data logger are normally unnecessary for tests that stay inside the modules' ratings. The N6700 control computer should continuously retrieve long-duration log data.

A separate precision digital multimeter is **optional rather than mandatory** for most line/load/range/thermal tests. Keep one available for independent verification, tighter measurement uncertainty, low-level circuit-node measurements and calibration cross-checks.

A separate instrument is still required for:

- microampere-level sleep and closed-circuit current;
- high-bandwidth ripple, ringing, startup, inrush and fast transient capture — use the digital oscilloscope;
- hard short-circuit switching and precise micro-interruptions — use a dedicated relay or transistor fault-insertion fixture;
- reverse-polarity, regenerative and four-quadrant battery behavior;
- forced output/backfeed tests requiring a source on the product output;
- ISO 7637 transient generation;
- CISPR 25, ISO 11452 and ISO 10605 electromagnetic-compatibility testing;
- humidity, vibration, mechanical shock, ingress, chemical and high-voltage qualification;
- temperature measurement unless supplied by the thermal chamber.

## Recommended execution order

1. Baseline characterization: PS-001 through PS-007.
2. Dynamic behavior: PS-008 through PS-013.
3. Protection and fault injection: PS-014 through PS-020.
4. Automotive supply profiles: AUTO-001 through AUTO-017, beginning with non-destructive ramps before severe pulses.
5. Temperature corners: TH-001 through TH-005 and TH-008.
6. Endurance: LIFE-001 through LIFE-003.
7. Formal transient, electromagnetic-compatibility and environmental qualification with compliant specialized equipment.
