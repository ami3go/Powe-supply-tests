# Automotive Power Supply Validation Test Matrix

_Last updated: 2026-09-20_

## Scope

This matrix is intended for validation of an automotive direct-current power supply, direct-current converter, or electronic control unit power stage using a programmable power supply, electronic load, oscilloscope and thermal chamber. It combines converter characterization, automotive battery disturbances, fault and protection tests, thermal and endurance testing, electromagnetic-compatibility and transient qualification, and 48-volt / voltage-class-B references.

> **Important:** this is a planning matrix, not a substitute for the licensed standard or customer or vehicle-manufacturer component requirement specification. Exact severity, waveform, source impedance, repetitions, functional-status requirement, sample count and acceptance limits shall be taken from the applicable contract specification and standard edition.

### Present setup status

- **Available** — can normally be executed with the present bench.
- **Partial** — useful pre-compliance testing is possible, but a compliant fixture, generator or instrument is needed for the full method.
- **Additional equipment required** — requires dedicated electromagnetic-compatibility, environmental, high-voltage or other specialized equipment, or an external laboratory.

The **Equipment required** column lists the complete practical setup for each test, not only equipment that is missing from the present bench.

## Recommended execution order with the current bench

1. **Baseline characterization:** PS-001 through PS-007.
2. **Dynamic behavior:** PS-008 through PS-013.
3. **Protection and fault injection:** PS-014 through PS-020.
4. **Automotive supply profiles:** AUTO-001 through AUTO-017, beginning with non-destructive ramps before severe pulses.
5. **Temperature corners:** TH-001 through TH-005 and TH-008, repeating the most important baseline and dynamic tests.
6. **Endurance:** LIFE-001 through LIFE-003 after short-duration validation has passed.
7. **Formal qualification:** conducted-transient, electromagnetic-compatibility and environmental tests with compliant equipment or an accredited laboratory.

## Current bench coverage and main equipment gaps

The present programmable power supply, programmable electronic load, digital oscilloscope and thermal chamber can cover a large part of the characterization, dynamic, protection and thermal matrix. The most useful additions for automotive power-electronics work are:

- precision digital multimeter and/or power analyzer;
- direct-current probe plus calibrated low-resistance current shunt;
- thermocouple and temperature-logging system;
- automated relay or transistor-based fault-insertion box;
- four-quadrant automotive battery simulator with arbitrary-waveform capability;
- ISO 7637 transient generator;
- CISPR 25 line-impedance-stabilization or artificial network and electromagnetic-interference receiver;
- bulk-current-injection radio-frequency generator, amplifier and injection probes;
- ISO 10605 electrostatic-discharge simulator;
- humidity chamber and mechanical-shaker access for environmental qualification.
